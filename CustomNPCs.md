# __**Custom NPCS**__
## The Breakroom (use !breakroom to get here)
- Barista
  - This NPC sells various food and drink to boost the player's stats.
  - Due to supply and demand the prices are always changing.<br>
 ![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1227fb0b-8866-484b-99cb-98e2aa19ccec)

- Moogle
  - This moogle just wanted to relax and drink coffee...
  - He will offer you the moghouse menu since you are nice.<br>
  ![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c1dc3fa-43ed-46fa-83eb-b0a68e784dc5)

 
- Homepoint Crystal
  - This is a special homepoint crystal that has all teleports unlocked
  - You will have to get a job to level 50 before it can be used.<br>
  ![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fe05115f-467c-410f-aa23-47136958ebfb)

 
- Runes
  - This chest is full of Maze Runes that can be purchased for enhancements.
